## The Evolutionary Milestone of Mondly's Journey

 **10 years ago today, Mondly was officially launched on the AppStore.**

 From a small startup with a big dream, we have grown into a global language learning platform trusted by millions. During this decade, we began to speak the language of innovation fluently, experienced significant growth, and remained steadfast in our commitment to making language learning fun, easy, and accessible for everyone.

 The journey was incredible, but it wouldn’t have been possible without our dedicated team, wonderful colleagues at Pearson, and passionate users like you. Your support and enthusiasm have been the driving force behind our success, inspiring us to continuously improve and expand our offerings.

 As we celebrate this significant milestone, we invite you to take a trip down memory lane with us. We’re excited to share a snapshot of our 10 years of success, highlighting the key moments and achievements that have defined our journey.

 Happy anniversary to everyone learning languages with Mondly! Here’s to many more years of breaking language barriers together. 🥂

#### **About Mondly**

 Mondly is a leading language learning platform that has been named “Regional App of the Year” by Facebook, “App of the Day” by Apple, and “Editors’ Choice” by Google Play. Launched in 2014, it quickly became a leading app in the mobile space. According to Statista, Mondly is currently in the Top 5 language learning apps worldwide by number of downloads.
